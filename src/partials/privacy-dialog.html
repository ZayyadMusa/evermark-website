Privacy & data

Your records stay with you.

Brand briefs, saved review links and access requests are stored on the server under your signed-in account. Other users cannot read or change them through this workspace. A saved brief does not submit an official application, and a saved link does not trigger automated scanning or a legal notice.

Access requests contain the email and service interest you provide. They are stored for review; submitting a request does not send an automated email. You can remove a request below, and remove saved brand briefs and links from The Shield. Account holders and authorised site administrators can access the records needed to operate the workspace.

This browser stores your current brand selection. Animations follow your device’s reduced-motion setting. The identity-studio name, type and category may be kept in session storage for up to 30 minutes while you sign in, then removed when the brief form is restored. Names carried between the identity studio and Filing appear after the # in the address and are not sent with the page request. Fonts, artwork and scripts are hosted with the site. Hosting and sign-in providers may process request and account information to operate the service.
