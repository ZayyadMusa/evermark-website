Can I submit a trademark application here?

You can prepare, save and download a brand brief. Official registry submission is not activated for this workspace. Use “Request official filing access” on the Filing page to register your interest. Saving a brief does not reserve a name or create legal rights.

Does one registration protect me worldwide?

Trademark protection is territorial. Where you need protection matters, and international routes involve designated countries or regions and their requirements. Read WIPO’s trademark overview.

Is filing the same as getting registered?

No. Filing starts an application process; it does not guarantee registration. The relevant office examines the application and other requirements may apply. Evermark’s saved brief is a preparation record. Explore the USPTO’s trademark basics.

Does The Shield scan marketplaces automatically?

Automated monitoring is not connected to this workspace yet. You can save real links, keep review notes, mark items reviewed or archived, and download those records. Request monitoring access from The Shield to register your interest.

Can I send a legal notice from the workspace?

No notice-delivery service is connected. The workspace records your evidence and decisions; it does not establish infringement, send cease-and-desist letters or submit takedowns. An actual case requires assessment of the rights, context and applicable law.

Where are my details saved, and can I remove them?

Briefs, saved links and access requests belong to your signed-in account and are stored on the server. Remove a brand or link in The Shield, and withdraw access requests through Privacy & data in the footer. Your current brand selection stays in this browser. Animations follow your device’s reduced-motion setting.
